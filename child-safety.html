DapUp Child Safety Standards

Applies to the DapUp app (iOS and Android) and all DapUp services, operated by DapUp Corp. Last updated: October 14, 2026.

Our standard: zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

DapUp strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) of any kind. This includes, without limitation:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM): any image, video, text or other content that sexually depicts or exploits a minor.
  • Grooming: befriending, contacting or manipulating a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Sexualizing minors in any way, including comments, messages, profiles or Moments.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, or any attempt to arrange contact with a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Sharing, requesting, promoting or linking to any of the above.

Accounts that violate these standards are permanently removed, and content is preserved and reported as required by law.

Who may use DapUp

DapUp is intended for adults. People under 18 may not create a DapUp account. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we remove it.

How to report

  • In the app: open the person's profile or chat, tap the menu (⋮) and choose Block or Report. Blocking stops all contact immediately.
  • By email: contact@dapup.app with the subject "Child safety report". Include the person's name as shown in DapUp and what happened. Do not send us illegal images.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 (or your local emergency number) first.
  • You can also report directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678.

What we do with a report

  • We review child safety reports as our highest priority.
  • We remove violating content and permanently ban the accounts involved.
  • We report apparent child sexual abuse material to NCMEC, as required by U.S. law, and cooperate with law enforcement.
  • We comply with all applicable child safety laws and regulations.
